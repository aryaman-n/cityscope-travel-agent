Tokyo is Japan's capital and its largest metropolitan center. It combines historic districts and shrines with dense commercial neighborhoods, contemporary architecture, and an extensive rail system. Common highlights include Sensō-ji in Asakusa, Meiji Shrine, the gardens around the Imperial Palace, the museums of Ueno, Shibuya Crossing, and the viewpoints in Shinjuku.

Rail and subway services make most districts accessible, while a stored-value transit card simplifies transfers. Distinct areas reward separate half-day visits: Yanaka for older streets, Ginza for shopping, Akihabara for electronics and pop culture, and Tsukiji's outer market for food. Etiquette on public transport is quiet and orderly. Seasonal draws include spring cherry blossoms and autumn foliage.
